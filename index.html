About

Runs on your device. Nothing is uploaded.

Perspective: JPEG and PNG, up to 4 MP and 2560 px. Saved copies drop EXIF and color profiles.

Fuji: RAF files are developed at half size. The film simulation looks are LUTs fitted from a Fujifilm camera's own output and approximate the camera, they are not Fujifilm's rendering.

Install

iPhone: Safari, Share, Add to Home Screen. Android: Install app.

Credits

Built on darktable's perspective code and LibRaw, compiled to WebAssembly, with film simulation LUTs by Bastian Bechtold. Independent, not affiliated with darktable, LibRaw or Fujifilm.
